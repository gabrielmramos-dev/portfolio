For a long time, I thought I could only write when I was sure.
When I knew enough.
When the risk of being wrong was zero.

That moment never comes.

Learning, building and thinking better means exposing the process, not just the result.
Keeping everything to myself has always meant forgetting it later.

This blog starts as a public record of learning.
To organize ideas, be wrong more clearly and grow with intention.

If something here makes sense to you, feel free to comment, disagree or add to it.
This space is as open as the process.
